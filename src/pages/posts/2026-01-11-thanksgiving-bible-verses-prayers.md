Last November, Karen's family faced their most difficult Thanksgiving yet. Her mother had just been diagnosed with cancer, her son had lost his job, and finances were tight. As they sat around a modest table, Karen suggested they each share one thing they were grateful for.

**What happened next surprised everyone. As each person spoke — health, togetherness, faith, a warm home — tears flowed freely. It was their most meaningful Thanksgiving in decades.**

## Why Gratitude Matters in the Bible

The Bible mentions thankfulness over 140 times. It's not just a nice habit — it's a spiritual discipline that transforms how we see God, ourselves, and our circumstances.

"Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good; his love endures forever." — Psalm 107:1 (NIV)

Gratitude shifts our focus from what we lack to what we have. It's an act of faith — trusting that God is good even when life is hard.

## 5 Thanksgiving Prayers

### Prayer Before a Thanksgiving Meal
*Heavenly Father, we gather around this table with grateful hearts. Thank You for the food before us, the family beside us, and the love between us. Bless the hands that prepared this meal. May we never take Your provision for granted. In Jesus' name, Amen.*

### Prayer for a Difficult Season
*Lord, even when life feels heavy, I choose to thank You. Thank You for being near to the brokenhearted. Thank You for carrying me through what I couldn't face alone. Help me find reasons to praise You today. Amen.*

### Prayer of General Gratitude
*Father, I thank You for the breath in my lungs, the roof over my head, and the grace that meets me each morning. Open my eyes to the blessings I overlook. Make me a person of gratitude. Amen.*

### Family Thanksgiving Prayer
*God of all good things, we thank You for our family — messy, imperfect, and deeply loved. Thank You for the memories we share and the ones still to come. Bind us together in Your love. Amen.*

### Prayer for Those in Need
*Compassionate God, as we celebrate abundance, we remember those who are hungry, lonely, and hurting. Move us to share what we have. Let our gratitude overflow into generosity. Amen.*

## Building a Year-Round Gratitude Practice

Thanksgiving isn't just a November holiday — it's a way of life.

1. **Keep a gratitude journal.** Write three things you're thankful for each night.
2. **Say it out loud.** Tell someone why you appreciate them this week.
3. **Thank God first.** Before asking God for anything, start your prayer with thanksgiving.
4. **Look for the hidden blessings.** Even hard seasons contain gifts — growth, compassion, deeper faith.
